## What you will make

Build a duck-rescue game. Swim around rocks, collect lost ducks, and dodge drifting hooks.

The duck stays near the centre while the scenery moves around it. Use the **arrow keys** to swim. Each rescued duck earns one point; touching a hook ends the game.

Try the finished game, then build your own using the starter beside the instructions.
